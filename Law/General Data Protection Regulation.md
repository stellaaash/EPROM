The GDPR is a data protection law, implemented in the [[European Union]] in May 2018.
Its goal is **to protect personal data, which is "any data associated with an individual that can be utilized to identify them"**.
# Key Points
- **Prior approval** must be obtained **before collecting** any personal data, including by AI systems.
- **Personal data** should be **kept at a minimum**, and only collected **when necessary**.
- Adequate measures need to be adopted to **protect stored personal data**.
# Jurisdiction
This law applies to **all businesses that operate in the European Union, and collect/store/process its citizens' personal data**.
# Fines
- Tier 1: For the severe infractions, like unintended data collection, sharing data with third parties without consent, etc.
  The maximum penalty for this is **4% of the organization's revenue, or 20 million euros, whichever is greater**.
  Tier 2: For less severe violations, like data breach or cyber policies breakage.
  The maximum fine for this is **2% of revenue, or 10 million euros, whichever is greater**.
