# Narrow AI
Also known as *specialized AI*, these systems are **specialized in specific tasks**. They can work in a **limited context, and are not able to learn or act beyond their programming**.
Examples include:
- Voice assistants like Siri or Alexa
- Translations tools like DeepL or Google Translate
- Image recognition systems, like facial recognitions
# Strong AI
Strong AI is a theorical concept of **systems that have human intelligence and are capable of performing a variety of tasks a human could also perform**.
This type of AI could learn, understand, and adapt to new situations in a similar manner as humans.
Strong AI doesn't exist yet, but is a goal of AI research.
