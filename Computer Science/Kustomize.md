Kustomize is a **tool that produces new [[Kubernetes]] from plain manifests, and *patching* them**.
# Kustomize Vs Helm
- It has no templating, contrary to [[Helm]], but also has no package-management functionality.
- Contrary to Helm, it doesn't need a separate tool, `kubectl` integrates it already.
