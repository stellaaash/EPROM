Machien learning is a sub-area of AI research. It **uses algorithms and models that learn from data to recognize patterns and make predictions or decisions**.
# Deep Learning
Deep learning is a **specialized form of machine learning based on deep [[Neural Network]]s**.
It has several layers of neurons, which enables the models to handle complex tasks such as speech processing, image recognition, and even autonomous driving.
