ISO 27001 is an **internationally recognized standard defining requirements for running an organization's Information Security Management System, or ISMS**.
It was developed by the [[International Organization for Standardization]], or ISO, and the [[International Electrotechnical Commission]], or IEC.
It **ensures that incident response policies are in place so that when those incidents do happen, they are handled accordingly**. The standard aims to reduce risk, but no standard is a perfect science, and incidents can still happen.
# Core Components
- **Scope**: the ISMS boundaries, including the covered assets and processes.
- **Information security policy**: A document that defines an organization's information security approach
- **Risk assessment**: Identifying and evaluating the risks to the CIA triad of the org's information
- **Risk treatment**: Selecting and implementing controls to mitigate the identified risks
- **Statement of Applicability (SoA)**: Document that specifies which controls from the standard are applicable, and which are not
- **Internal audit**: Conducting periodic audits of the ISMS to ensure that it is operating correctly
- **Management review**: Review the ISMS's performance regularly
